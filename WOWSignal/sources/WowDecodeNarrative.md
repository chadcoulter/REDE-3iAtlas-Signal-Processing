## Attempts, Failed Readings, Corrections, and Unresolved Leaps

The extraction was not a straight progression from `6EQUJ5` to `Look there`. We explored candidate interpretations, found inconsistencies, revised operators, and changed the directional reading. These steps matter because they show how the method changed in response to what we observed.

**Early ART reading.** Before settling on `ΑΣΤΡΑ` (`ASTRA`), we tried an `ART` reading. It was an exploratory interpretation, not the established result. The available reconstruction does not preserve the exact transformations that led us to reject or supersede it. Codex should treat it as an earlier candidate and look for its original trace.

**Premature punctuation assignment.** We attempted the mapping `Ϝς = .` and then withdrew it because the transformation had not been established. The attempted punctuation mapping must not be silently included as a valid operator. Its rejection is part of the record.

**Weak Rho mapping in ASTRA.** In the candidate `ASTRA` reading, the Rho correspondence was less constrained than the other character relationships. We recognized this weakness during the investigation. The retained `ΑΣΤΡΑ` reading was therefore a working interpretation, not proof that every letter had a uniquely determined mapping.

**Reinterpreting Digamma.** We initially described Digamma as *returning*. We corrected this to *reverse traversal to recover direction*. That distinction affects execution: Codex must preserve the difference between merely returning to a previous position and performing a reverse reading of the path. The corrected meaning is the one carried into the later directional analysis.

**Taurus prediction versus coordinate result.** The nested reading led us to consider Taurus as the reversed direction. We then performed the actual antipodal coordinate calculation. It yielded positions in Gemini, not Taurus. The computed result superseded the predicted location. Both the failed prediction and the calculation belong in the history.

**Galactic anticenter mismatch.** We explored the possible relationship to the Galactic anticenter. The reversed positions were approximately 22 degrees away. This was not a direct alignment. The offset must be retained as a measured discrepancy rather than reframed as a match.

**Outward versus inward reading of `Look there`.** We first treated the interpreted phrase as pointing outward toward an astronomical direction, then considered an inward or terrestrial reading. This was a change of interpretation, not an independent new signal measurement. The source calculation and each interpretive branch should remain distinguishable.

The surviving reconstruction does not establish how many other trials, unsuccessful transformations, or discarded possibilities occurred in the full original chat. These are the specific attempts and corrections currently identified; they should not be presented as an exhaustive experimental log.
