## Scope and selection rules

Inputs are Step 1's raw grid, numeric grid, cell records, and provenance manifest. Their hashes are verified before extraction. The original printout image, handwriting, circles, the handwritten word “Wow!”, and annotations are not opened or used by the script.

The earlier conversational suggestion to select marked rows was withdrawn after Chad clarified the scope. It was not executed or committed as a traversal result. This run replaces that proposal with uniform source-grid rules.

Treat the entire 82-row × 50-channel grid as the retained source. Preserve each space, numerical bin, and row/channel position. No threshold, target phrase, or annotation selects a row.

| Family | Rule | Forward paths |
|---|---|---:|
| H | Every row, left to right | 82 |
| V | Every column, top to bottom | 50 |
| DR | Every maximal down-right diagonal | 131 |
| DL | Every maximal down-left diagonal | 131 |
| Total | All four families | 394 |

Each forward path has an exact reverse partner, giving **788 ordered paths**. Four forward diagonals contain only one cell; those are retained and explicitly counted rather than silently removed.

Diagonal starts are ordered along the top edge from left to right, then down the applicable side, excluding a duplicated corner. Maximal means continue in that direction until the edge of the grid.

## What was saved

- [paths.json](run-001/paths.json): all paths, source-cell IDs, original character strings, numeric sequences, start/end coordinates, direction, and reverse partner.
- [intersections.csv](run-001/intersections.csv): every cell's horizontal, vertical, and two diagonal memberships, with its forward and reverse offsets.
- [triples.csv](run-001/triples.csv): all consecutive three-cell windows with path IDs, offsets, three source-cell IDs, and their three numeric values.
- [triple_counts.csv](run-001/triple_counts.csv): occurrence counts of numerical triples over forward paths.
- [summary.json](run-001/summary.json): counts, checks, and unresolved questions.
- [manifest.json](run-001/manifest.json): input, implementation, and output hashes.

Source-cell ID is `row_0based * 50 + channel_0based`. Positions join directly to Step 1's cell records and printed metadata. Spaces remain spaces in the raw character strings, and remain numeric bin zero in the numerical sequences.

Straight paths have zero internal direction changes. Reversal changes the sign of the direction vector. No composite path turning at an intersection was selected in this pass.
