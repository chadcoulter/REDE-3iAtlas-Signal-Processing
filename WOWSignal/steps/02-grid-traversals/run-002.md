## Candidate B — The printed E matches the transversal formula

The original intensity code `E` is present in the source and represents bin 14. In a declared upright block-letter model, it has:

```text

V + H_T + H_M + H_B
```

This exactly matches the structural description at the **glyph-representation level**. It is a possible alternative to reading those names as three selected time rows.

The three trunk/bar intersections have degrees **2, 3, 2**. Thus the model does not produce three degree-three junctions. A left/right reflection gives a backward E; top/bottom reflection preserves the E geometry. Neither operation produces ΛΤϜ by itself.

**What fits:** the four-component formula has a direct representation-level referent in a recorded source character.

**What does not follow:** the model is not a measured waveform geometry, proof of transmitted writing, a recovered nine-position matrix, or evidence that the original exploration used these strokes. It conflicts with a literal neighboring-measurement reading of the narrative. No glyph stroke is deleted to manufacture Digamma.

## Constraints that eliminate simplistic reconstructions

- Choosing three of the six event rows gives **20** row selections before choosing widths or directions. The source alone does not single out one choice.
- Six original samples have two middle positions, not a unique central sample.
- Any sum of three raw bins in this dataset is at most **90**. Therefore a rule that simply sums three raw cells cannot directly produce numeral Tau = 300. This excludes that arithmetic rule only; Tau could instead label a traversal operation.
- The current encoder uses **Stigma Ϛ**, not **Digamma Ϝ**, for six. No substitution between those glyphs is authorized by numeric equivalence alone.
- The phrase-level operation sequence and literal numeral values are distinct types. A description of operations could have five stages applied to six inputs; that would require an operational derivation, not one-to-one substitution.
